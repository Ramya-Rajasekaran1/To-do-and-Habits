## Design Context

### Users
- **Target Audience**: Creatives, designers, developers, and goal-oriented power users organizing daily time blocks, to-dos, and recurring habits.
- **Context of Use**: Rapid morning planning, day-long calendar time-blocking, and satisfying micro-logging of habit streaks.
- **Jobs to be Done**: Plan the day visually, drag unscheduled tasks into clear time slots, build consistency through habit tracking, and celebrate completed milestones.

### Brand Personality
- **Three-Word Voice**: Bold, Tactile, Punchy.
- **Tone**: High-energy, motivating, playful yet razor-sharp and structured.
- **Emotional Goals**: Clarity, momentum, delight, and a tangible sense of accomplishment.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual Style**: Cyber Neo-Brutalism (high-contrast black borders, hard-edge offset drop shadows, electric accent swatches on a warm canvas).
- **Inspirations**: Gumroad Retro, shadcn-neobrutalism, arcade quest interfaces.
- **Anti-References**: Generic pastel SaaS templates, blurry low-contrast glassmorphism, faint gray hairline borders, floating corporate illustrations.
- **Color Tokens**:
  - Brand Primary: Cyber Yellow (`#FFE135` / `#FFE600`)
  - Accent Pop: Acid Pink (`#FF3CAC` / `#FF69B4`)
  - Actions & Links: Cobalt Blue (`#2563EB`)
  - Success & Streaks: Emerald Green (`#16A34A`)
  - Background Canvas: Warm Off-White (`#FAFAF5`)
  - Inks & Strokes: Deep Solid Black (`#111111`)

### Design Principles
1. **Tangible Physicality**: Buttons and cards feel physical. Solid `2.5px solid #111` borders, distinct `4px 4px 0 #111` drop shadows, and satisfying tactile press-down states (`translate(2px, 2px)` with reduced shadow).
2. **Uncompromising Visual Contrast**: High legibility with bold geometric typography (`Archivo Black` headings, `Space Mono` metadata, `Archivo` body text).
3. **Ergonomic Spacing & Layout**: Clear column balance between time-block calendar grid and unscheduled queue, with minimum 44px touch targets for mobile.
4. **Purposeful Micro-Interactions**: Visual feedback on every hover, click, drag, and complete, paired with respectful `prefers-reduced-motion` fallbacks.
